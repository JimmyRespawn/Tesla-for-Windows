Provided by the user in FBX-20261001T111232Z-1-001.zip on 2026-10-01.
Original archive includes SUV, Taxi, SportsCar, Cop, SportsCar2, NormalCar1 and NormalCar2.

Runtime files: NormalCar1.fbx, NormalCar2.fbx, SUV.fbx and SportsCar2.fbx.
These source FBX files are retained unchanged. Model3TrafficAssets applies runtime
orientation/scale normalization to -X forward, Y-up, meter dimensions, maps the
materials to neutral gray body/dark glass/trim, smooths normals within a crease
angle, batches parts by material and adds local feathered contact shadows.
The models are generic vehicles by Quaternius, not official Tesla UI assets.

Vehicle templates and materials are shared between instances. No online model
requests, source textures or credential access are required.
